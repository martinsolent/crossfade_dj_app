CROSSFADE

Two decks, a mixer and four built-in tracks. Sound starts when you press the button.

On iPhone, switch off silent mode to hear it.

Track library

Your files stay on this device. Tempo is estimated when a file loads.

How to use it

  • Jog wheel: drag the centre to scratch. Drag the outer ring while playing to nudge the beat. Hold Shift and drag to search fast.
  • Cue: while paused it sets the cue point; at the cue point, hold to preview. While playing it jumps back and stops.
  • Beat Sync: matches this deck's tempo and beat to the other deck. Shift + Beat Sync changes the tempo fader range (±8, ±16, ±50%).
  • Loop: In and Out set a manual loop. 4 Beat starts a four-beat loop or exits the current one; ½× and 2× resize it. Shift + 4 Beat returns to the last loop.
  • Pads: Hot Cue stores eight jump points (Shift + pad clears one). Pad FX holds loop rolls, echo, reverb and filters. Beat Jump skips by beats. Sampler fires one-shots.
  • Mixer: Trim, three-band EQ and a filter per channel, channel faders, crossfader. Double-tap a knob or fader to reset it.
  • Beat FX: pick the effect, the beat length and the channel, set Depth, then switch it on.
  • Waveforms: tap or drag the thin overview bar to jump through a track. Tap the track name to load another.
  • Keyboard: Z play, X cue, S sync for deck 1. M play, N cue, K sync for deck 2.

There is one audio output, so there is no separate headphone cue.
